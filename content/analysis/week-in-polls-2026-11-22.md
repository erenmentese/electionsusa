Six national surveys finished fieldwork between November 16 and November 22. They gave ten results in the races we track, and all ten ended on Monday, November 16. The largest move was in the Republican primary, where JD Vance's average fell 0.6 points. Trump approval slipped 0.2 points and Kamala Harris gained 0.2 in the Democratic primary. The matchup averages did not move.

## Trump approval: 37.4%

The [Trump approval average](/approval/trump/) fell 0.2 points to 37.4% approve. Five new polls ended this week, all among registered or likely voters. Reuters/Ipsos was the lowest, at 34% approving and 65% disapproving. RMG Research for the Napolitan Institute was the highest, at 39% and 58%. Emerson College and The Economist/YouGov both had 37% approving, and Echelon Insights had 36%. Four of the five were below the average.

## The 2028 primaries: Vance down, Harris steady

Two polls of the Republican primary ended this week, and they disagreed. Emerson College had Marco Rubio at 44%, JD Vance at 24% and Ron DeSantis at 9%. Echelon Insights had Vance at 40%, Rubio at 26% and DeSantis at 12%, among 397 likely voters. The Emerson result pulled the [Republican primary average](/2028/republican-primary/) down. Vance still leads it, at 36.3%, down 0.6 points.

Three polls of the Democratic primary ended this week. Echelon Insights had Kamala Harris at 19%, Pete Buttigieg at 15% and Gavin Newsom at 11%. On Point Polling had Harris at 31% and Newsom at 13%, among 490 registered voters. Emerson College had Harris, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Buttigieg close together, all between 13.8% and 15.5%. The [Democratic primary average](/2028/democratic-primary/) rose 0.2 points. Harris leads it at 21.8%.

Primary polling this far out mostly measures name recognition. See [who is running for president in 2028](/2028/candidates/) for what each possible candidate has said.

## Matchups: no new polls

No new polls of a hypothetical 2028 general election ended this week. The averages are unchanged: [Vance vs. Newsom](/2028/president/vance-vs-newsom/) at Newsom +3.4, [Vance vs. Ocasio-Cortez](/2028/president/vance-vs-ocasio-cortez/) at Vance +1.5, and [Vance vs. Buttigieg](/2028/president/vance-vs-buttigieg/) at Buttigieg +2.8.

## What to watch

The two Republican primary polls this week were far apart, and one poll does not make a trend. The next releases from the regular national pollsters will show whether the Emerson result is an outlier. On the 2028 calendar, the next milestone is still the first formal campaign launch; see [when 2028 candidates are likely to announce](/analysis/when-will-2028-presidential-candidates-announce/).
