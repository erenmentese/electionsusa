The second week after the midterms brought few new polls. Three national surveys that finished fieldwork between November 9 and November 15 gave five results in the races we track. Two averages moved by 0.2 points. The rest did not move.

## Trump approval: 37.2%

The [Trump approval average](/approval/trump/) fell 0.2 points to 37.2% approve. Two new polls ended this week. The Economist/YouGov had 32% approving and 63% disapproving among registered voters. Reuters/Ipsos had 36% and 63%. The YouGov reading was the lower of the two, and both were below the average. The approval average uses a 14-day half-life, so it reacts faster than the others, but two polls were not enough to move it much.

## The 2028 primaries: a small dip for Vance

Two polls of the Republican primary ended this week. The Economist/YouGov had JD Vance at 40%, Marco Rubio at 33% and Ron DeSantis at 14%. Rasmussen Reports had Vance at 32%, Rubio at 27% and DeSantis at 13%. Both are small samples, at 366 and 384 likely voters. The [Republican primary average](/2028/republican-primary/) still has Vance ahead, at 37.5%, down 0.2 points.

The same Economist/YouGov survey asked Democratic primary voters. It had Kamala Harris at 20%, Gavin Newsom at 13% and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez at 12%, among 489 likely voters. The [Democratic primary average](/2028/democratic-primary/) did not move. Harris leads it at 22.2%.

Primary polling this far out mostly measures name recognition. No major candidate formally declared this week; see [who is running for president in 2028](/2028/candidates/) for what each has said.

## Matchups: no new polls

No new polls of a hypothetical 2028 general election ended this week. The averages are unchanged: [Vance vs. Newsom](/2028/president/vance-vs-newsom/) at Newsom +3.4, [Vance vs. Ocasio-Cortez](/2028/president/vance-vs-ocasio-cortez/) at Vance +1.5, and [Vance vs. Buttigieg](/2028/president/vance-vs-buttigieg/) at Buttigieg +2.8.
