How this is scored

This is the same method as the historical pollster scorecard, applied to the national generic ballot: every poll that finished fieldwork in the final 21 days, compared with the national House popular vote. Some districts have no contested race, which pulls the national House vote away from pure party preference, so even a perfect generic-ballot poll can miss by a point or two. We publish a preliminary score from the unofficial count and update it, with the date, when the count changes and again when states certify.
