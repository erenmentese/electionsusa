How this map works

Every state starts with its certified {{ year }} margin. The swing slider adds the same number of points toward one party in every state, a "uniform swing": the simplest way to ask what a better or worse national environment would do to the map. Real elections never move every state equally, so tap any state to call it yourself.

2028 uses the same electoral votes as {{ year }}: both follow the House apportionment from the 2020 census, and the next reapportionment, after the 2030 census, takes effect in 2032.
