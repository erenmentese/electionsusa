Reading the gap

A nomination market price is not the same quantity as a primary poll share. A candidate polling at 20% in a crowded field can be a 50% favourite if traders expect the field to consolidate around them. Markets also price events that polls can't see: whether a candidate runs at all, health, scandals, endorsements. Treat large gaps as a prompt to ask why, not as proof that one side is wrong.

Prices are mid-market or last-trade values at the time of our snapshot and can move within minutes. Nothing on this page is investment advice. Prices are read from Polymarket's public API; contracts that are closed, inactive or have traded less than $10,000 are skipped. Kalshi lists similar markets; we link to it rather than republish its prices. Historical Polymarket snapshots are in the open dataset; the prices remain subject to Polymarket's terms.
