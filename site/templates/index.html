What this is

ElectionsUSA tracks public opinion ahead of the November 7, 2028 United States presidential election. It averages polls with a published, reproducible method; it keeps score of how close each pollster's final polls came to actual results; and it puts prediction-market prices next to the polls so readers can see when traders and survey respondents disagree.

The site is independent, carries no campaign, party or government affiliation, and is funded by advertising. The complete dataset is downloadable and every figure links back to the pollster that published it.
