Pollster scorecard

How accurate is each pollster?

We recompute simple, explainable accuracy statistics for every pollster with at least ten polls in the final three weeks of a race since 2016, using FiveThirtyEight's open archive of 11,000+ polls matched to results. FiveThirtyEight's final letter grade (2023) is shown alongside for reference; it is no longer updated since the site closed in 2025.

How to read this table

  • Avg error: mean absolute difference, in points, between the poll's margin and the actual margin. Lower is better. A typical final-three-weeks poll misses by about 5 points.
  • Adjusted error: the same figure shrunk toward the field average (k = 15 polls), so pollsters with few polls aren't ranked on luck. Rows are sorted by this column.
  • Bias: average signed error. D+ means the pollster's polls were, on average, more favourable to Democrats than the result; R+ more favourable to Republicans.
  • Right call: share of polls whose leader won.
