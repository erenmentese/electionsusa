Embed

You may embed our SVG charts or reproduce tables with attribution and a link. A copy-paste embed widget is planned.

Schema

poll_id, race_id, pollster, sponsor, start_date, end_date, sample_size, population,
candidate, pct, source_url, source_type, notes, fetched_at

population is A (adults), RV (registered voters) or LV (likely voters). source_type is seed (entered by hand from a cited report), wikipedia (parsed from a compilation table that cites the pollster) or manual.
